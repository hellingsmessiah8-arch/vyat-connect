Who We Are VYAT Connect Agribusiness Initiative operates under VYAT Ventures, a youth-led agribusiness innovation platform in Malawi focused on innovating, empowering and transforming rural agricultural systems. We work to connect farmers, markets, finance and enterprise development into one simple and functional system that improves how agriculture is practiced and how value is created. Our goal is to make agriculture more organized, profitable, and accessible. Especially for smallholder farmers, youth and women
