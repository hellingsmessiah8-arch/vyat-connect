ABOUT VYAT CONNECT

Building A Sustainable Agricultural Future

VYAT Connect Agribusiness Initiative under VYAT Ventures is dedicated to transforming agriculture in Malawi by connecting farmers to markets, promoting climate-smart agriculture, and creating sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To enhance commercialization and market participation among smallholder farmers through innovation, collaboration, and sustainable agribusiness solutions.

Our Vision

A prosperous agricultural ecosystem where smallholder farmers thrive through inclusive, resilient, and market-driven agriculture.
